About Gaza For All

A movement built to last, not a moment to win

Who we are, why we exist, and what we intend to be regardless of any single election.

Who We Are

An independent civic-political movement

Gaza For All organizes citizens across Nasarawa around a shared civic purpose: inclusive participation, responsible leadership and accountable governance. Gaza For All supports Hon. Jonathan Gaza Gbefwi, but Gaza For All is not the same thing as the candidate, and it is not a political party.

Why We Exist

Because citizens deserve a structured voice between elections, not just during them

Politics in Nasarawa should not depend on a single candidate or a single season. Gaza For All exists to keep people organized, heard and informed — continuously.

Aspiration

Vision

A Nasarawa where every community — indigene and non-indigene, of every faith — shares equally in opportunity, development and peace.

Position

Mission

To organize citizens, carry community concerns to leadership, support principled and inclusive governance, and hold that leadership publicly accountable.

Values

What guides how we work

Inclusion

Everyone who contributes to Nasarawa deserves a voice in its future.

Integrity

We say what we mean, and we do not fabricate claims, statistics or endorsements.

Accountability

Support must never eliminate questions, evidence or public responsibility.

What “For All” Means

Not indigenes versus non-indigenes — belonging, contribution and a shared future

Everyone who contributes to Nasarawa deserves a voice in its future. That is the whole of what “For All” means to us.

Gaza For All

Unity. Development. Opportunity. Peace. Accountability. Building a greater Nasarawa together.
